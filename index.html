About

I am a Ph.D. student in Political Science at The Pennsylvania State University. My research focuses on authoritarian politics and political institutions, with projects on elections, protest and state responses, party organization, and authoritarian legacies.

Research

Elections and State Concessions to Anti-government Mobilisation in Authoritarian Regimes

Revise and resubmit at Government and Opposition

This project examines how the timing of elections shapes authoritarian rulers' concessionary responses to anti-government mobilisation.

The Transition of Authoritarian Ruling Parties and Women's Cabinet Representation after Democratization

Work in progress on authoritarian party legacies and women's representation in post-authoritarian cabinets.

Populism in Authoritarian Regimes? Party Origins, Economic Shocks, and Populist Rhetoric

Work in progress on the conditions under which authoritarian ruling parties employ populist rhetoric.
